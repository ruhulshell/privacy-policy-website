2. Information We Collect and How It Is Handled

Mumin Habit Sunnah And Duas is designed with a local-first approach to user privacy:

  • Local Data Storage: Your daily habit checklists, routine progress, streaks, and personal notes are stored locally on your device (such as via local SQLite storage). We do not upload your personal tracking logs to our own external servers.
  • App Content: The app includes structured reference content such as Sunnah habits, Duas, Hadiths, Quranic verses/reflections, Names of Allah, and daily guidance. Viewing this content does not require submitting personal profile information.
  • Audio Playback: When you play audio clips or recitations within the app, playback occurs locally on your device or via standard media playback without linking audio activity to a personal account profile.

3. Permissions and Device Access

Mumin Habit Sunnah And Duas only accesses device capabilities needed for core app functionality, such as local storage/preferences to save your progress and audio output for media playback. It does not require access to your contacts, precise GPS location, camera, or microphone unless explicitly stated for a specific feature.

5. Children's Privacy
